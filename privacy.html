Privacy policy

How we handle your data.

Plain-English version of the legal stuff. Local-first by default; BYO-LLM keeps inference data on your side.

Last updated: June 9, 2026 Effective: June 9, 2026 Operator: The SHORT UX Co., Ottawa, Canada

1. Who we are

Accessor is a product of The SHORT UX Co., an Ontario-incorporated UX consultancy based in Ottawa, Canada. When this policy says "we" or "us" it means The SHORT UX Co. operating the Accessor product. Contact: ryan@shortux.com.

2. What we collect

Accessor comes in two forms with different data footprints. Pick the one that describes your install:

Local-first (self-hosted) deployment

  • None of your scan data leaves your machine. Audit URLs, scan results, findings, screenshots, and reports all live on the device you ran Accessor on.
  • Your license file stays on your machine. We do not phone home to check or revoke licenses.
  • What we know about you: only what you've told us directly (email, organization name, GitHub username if you provided one to be added as a repo collaborator).

SaaS deployment (accessor.app and similar hosted versions)

  • Account identity: email address and display name from your Google or Microsoft sign-in. We do not request additional scopes.
  • Workspace data: workspace name, tier, team members you've invited, clients you've created, audits you've run, findings produced.
  • BYO-LLM keys: stored encrypted at rest in our database, used only to call your chosen LLM provider on your behalf. We never read them back to your browser.
  • Billing data: when subscriptions ship, payment method and billing address handled by Stripe — we never see your full card number.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • Telemetry from your audits. We do not collect usage analytics, error reports, or behavioural tracking from inside the audit tool itself.
  • Tracking cookies. Our marketing site does not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • LLM inference content. When you use BYO-LLM, your prompts and your audit content go directly from Accessor to your chosen LLM provider's API. Accessor's servers see the metadata (which workspace, which audit) but the content of the LLM exchanges is handled by your provider under their privacy terms.
  • Sensitive personal information. Accessor is not designed for health, biometric, or financial data and we ask you not to use it for that.

4. Where your data lives

For the SaaS version, customer data is hosted on Supabase, with the database region selected at workspace creation time. Canadian customers can request a Canadian-region Supabase project for data residency, which keeps account and workspace data inside Canada.

LLM API calls go to the provider you configured (Anthropic, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI Canada Central, or your own endpoint). The residency of that traffic is governed by your provider's terms.

For local-first deployments, all data lives where you ran the tool. We have no visibility into it.

5. Who we share data with

  • Sub-processors strictly necessary to operate the service: Supabase (database + auth), Stripe (when billing ships), Vercel (frontend hosting). Each is contractually bound to handle data only as instructed by us.
  • Your chosen LLM provider for inference calls when BYO-LLM is configured.
  • Your team members within your workspace, scoped by their role (admin / member / viewer).
  • No advertisers or data brokers. We do not sell, rent, or otherwise transfer customer data to third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Legal compliance: if compelled by valid Canadian legal process, we will respond, attempting to notify you first unless prohibited by law.

6. How long we keep it

Account and workspace data is retained while your account is active and for 30 days after deletion to allow restoration in case of accidental cancellation. After 30 days, data is permanently deleted from our active systems and purged from backups within 90 days.

Audit data is retained at your discretion — you can delete individual audits, engagements, or clients at any time from the dashboard.

7. Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial legislation, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in machine-readable form
  • Withdraw consent for processing (effectively, close your account)

To exercise any of these rights, email ryan@shortux.com with the subject "Privacy request" and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Security

  • Database access uses row-level security (RLS), isolating each workspace's data to its members only.
  • API keys and other secrets are stored encrypted at rest in our database.
  • All traffic between your browser and our servers uses TLS.
  • OAuth sign-in via Google or Microsoft, no passwords stored.
  • The local-first version does not transmit data over the network at all (other than fetching the target URL you're auditing and calling your configured LLM provider).

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability, please email ryan@shortux.com directly. We'll respond within 72 hours.

9. Children

Accessor is a B2B tool not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll post material changes to this page and, if you have a SaaS account, notify you by email. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.

Mailing address available on request.
